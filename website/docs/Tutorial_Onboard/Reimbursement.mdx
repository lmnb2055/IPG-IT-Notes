# Expense Reimbursement: Employee ACH

How to get reimbursed for out-of-pocket IT purchases.

> **💡 Good to know:** Reimbursements under $200 are prepared with a Claude skill that fills in the ACH form for you. Anything over $200 goes through Accounting instead. Either way, the IT manager signs before it's sent.

> **⚠️ Do not start without the receipt as a PDF, with the total clearly visible.** A card statement line doesn't count.

## Under $200

1. Collect your receipt as a PDF with the total clearly visible.
2. Go to Team Claude and run `/02-tracy-it-ach-reimbursement`.
3. Answer the two prompts:

   | Prompt | What to enter |
   | --- | --- |
   | Who's submitting this? | Your name, for example *Sidney Chen* |
   | What was this for? | One short line, for example *IT team lunch – East Hanover* |

4. Check what comes back: name, amount, purpose, and that the receipt is attached.
5. Download it and send it to the IT manager for signature.

> **Note:** The skill only fills in the form. Nothing is submitted until you email the signed packet.

## Over $200

1. Attach the Recommendation Report from your supervisor.
2. Email iphqap@inteplast.com with Tracy cc'd.

> **⚠️ Codes:** 0130 and IPHQ are the IT/HQ codes. Other departments and plants use different ones, so check with the Accounting department.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| Wrong name or amount on the form | Re-run the skill with corrected input. |
| Receipt missing from the packet | Re-upload it as a PDF and re-run the skill. |
| Accounts payable returns the request | Usually a missing code or signature. Complete it and resubmit. |
