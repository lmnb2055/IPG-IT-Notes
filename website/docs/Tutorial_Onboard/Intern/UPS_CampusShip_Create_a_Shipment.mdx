# UPS CampusShip: Create a Shipment

How to ship IT equipment to another site or a user through UPS CampusShip.

> **💡 Good to know:** CampusShip is UPS's corporate shipping portal. IT uses it to send laptops, peripherals, and replacement hardware between sites and to remote users. Shipping costs are billed back internally, so the **Reference #1** field matters: it's how the charge is allocated.

## Before You Start

Have the recipient's full name, address, and phone number ready. You can't create the shipment without them.

## Steps

### 1. Sign in

1. Go to [UPS CampusShip](https://www.campusship.ups.com/).
2. Sign in with **jcheng@inteplast.com**.

{/* Screenshot: image-20261007-200507.png */}

### 2. Enter the shipment details

{/* Screenshot: fc2f63fe-e7c5-4502-9934-187b1b9658cb.png */}

1. Fill in the recipient's information.
   {/* Screenshot: image-20261007-201500.png */}
2. Weigh the package and round up to the nearest whole number.
   {/* Screenshots: 7030dd8b-2de9-4d4d-9ed6-7cb3e9810249.png, image-20261007-201532.png */}
3. Choose **Ground** service and enable email notifications.
   {/* Screenshot: image-20261007-202150.png */}
4. Confirm the **Reference #1** field (used for cost allocation).

### 3. Add notification emails

Under **Additional Shipping Options**, add email addresses for notifications:

- Your own
- The recipient's
- The recipient's supervisor, if needed

{/* Screenshot: image-20261007-202441.png */}

### 4. Review and print

1. Review the shipment details.
   {/* Screenshot: image-20261007-204347.png */}
2. Print the shipping documents and attach the label to the package.
   {/* Screenshot: image-20261007-205418.png */}

**Expected result:** The label is printed and attached, and notification emails are sent to everyone listed.
