# UPS CampusShip: Create a Shipment

How to ship IT equipment to another site or a user through UPS CampusShip.

> **💡 Good to know:** CampusShip is UPS's corporate shipping portal. IT uses it to send laptops, peripherals, and replacement hardware between sites and to remote users. Shipping costs are billed back internally, so the **Reference #1** field matters: it's how the charge is allocated.

## Before You Start

Have the recipient's full name, address, and phone number ready. You can't create the shipment without them.

## Steps

### 1. Sign in

1. Go to [UPS CampusShip](https://www.campusship.ups.com/).
2. Sign in with **jcheng@inteplast.com**.

![UPS sign-in page with the username field filled in](./image-20261007-200507.png)

### 2. Enter the shipment details

1-2. Fill in the recipient's information.

   ![Where is this shipment going? section with the recipient's address](./fc2f63fe-e7c5-4502-9934-187b1b9658cb.png)

   ![Address book save options and UPS Access Point checkbox](./image-20261007-201500.png)

3. Weigh the package and round up to the nearest whole number.

   ![What are you shipping? section with the package weight and declared value](./7030dd8b-2de9-4d4d-9ed6-7cb3e9810249.png)

4. Choose **Ground** service and enable email notifications.

   ![UPS Ground Service selected with Send E-mail Notifications checked](./image-20261007-201532.png)

5-7. Confirm the **Reference #1** field (used for cost allocation), then select **Next**.

   ![Reference #1 field, payment method, and the Next button](./image-20261007-202150.png)

### 3. Add notification emails

Under **Additional Shipping Options**, add email addresses for notifications:

- Your own
- The recipient's
- The recipient's supervisor, if needed

![Additional Shipping Options page with notification emails entered](./image-20261007-202441.png)

### 4. Review and print

1. Review the shipment details.

   ![Review Your Shipment Details page showing the addresses](./image-20261007-204347.png)

2. Print the shipping documents and attach the label to the package.

   ![Print Shipping Documents with Label and Receipt checked](./image-20261007-205418.png)

**Expected result:** The label is printed and attached, and notification emails are sent to everyone listed.
